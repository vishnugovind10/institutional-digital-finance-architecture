# Tokenized-asset treasury

1. A treasury mandate sets eligible assets, concentration limits, liquidity horizon, and accountable owner.
2. Authority validates the operator or delegated agent and transaction scope.
3. Eligibility and valuation controls establish asset status, price time, haircut, and custody constraints.
4. Economic decisioning evaluates use, cost, risk, and funding alternatives.
5. Transaction authorization binds the approved intent and limits.
6. Execution records venue outcomes; settlement coordinates asset and cash legs.
7. Financial state updates positions and obligations only from appropriately qualified events.
8. Liquidity and exposure are recomputed for required time buckets.
9. Internal, custody, venue, and accounting records are reconciled.
10. Assurance packages evidence and open exceptions for an institutional decision.

Relevant independent examples include Institutional Digital Collateral Stack, Haircut, Institutional Settlement State Machine, NavBridge, and Institutional Digital Asset Assurance. This workflow is a proposed composition; those repositories are not integrated.
