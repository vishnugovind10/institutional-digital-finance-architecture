# Economic decisioning

Decisioning asks whether an authorized action is suitable under the current mandate, policy, expected cost, risk, liquidity, timing, and available alternatives. Apply hard constraints before ranking eligible options. Preserve inputs, assumptions, exclusions, reason codes, and the decision time so a later reviewer can reproduce what was assessed.

A route or asset that passes policy can still be uneconomic; a favorable score cannot override a hard limit. Decision approval is time-bounded and should be reconsidered when material inputs change. Decision records are distinct from execution evidence.
