# Assurance and evidence

Assurance asks whether available evidence supports a bounded claim about the lifecycle. Evidence records should identify issuer/observer, subject, claim, scope, source, creation and effective times, integrity method, freshness, and limitations. Distinguish independently recomputed facts from issuer assertions and trusted dependencies.

A missing or stale artifact is unknown, not success. Report exceptions, observer visibility, and evidence gaps alongside conclusions. Assurance is a decision-support layer, not proof of regulatory compliance, production security, or complete system correctness.

See Institutional Digital Asset Assurance, Canton ProofPack, Liquid ProofPack, and Agentic Financial Consequence for different evidence boundaries.
