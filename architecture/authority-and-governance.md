# Authority and governance

Authority answers **who may perform which action, for which principal, on what scope, until when, under whose delegation, and with what revocation path?** A valid credential is only one input. The control record should bind actor and principal identities, action/resource scope, limits, validity, delegation chain, policy version, and decision time.

Separate standing mandate, delegated authority, transaction-specific authorization, and economic approval. Recheck revocation and expiry at the enforcement boundary. Record denial reasons and the policy/evidence versions used. A successful authorization must not be presented as proof that execution, settlement, or resulting exposure stayed within mandate.
