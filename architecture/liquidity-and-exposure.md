# Liquidity and exposure

Assess funding against obligation amount, currency, location, and due time. A solvent or adequately collateralized position may still fail a time-specific liquidity constraint. Model intraday timing, synchronized demand, settlement windows, haircuts, concentration, and contingency funding as explicit inputs.

Aggregate exposures across agents, venues, assets, counterparties, and pending actions. Individually permissible actions can jointly exceed a mandate. Report what was aggregated, what was omitted, and whether the result is observed or scenario-derived.

See Temporal Liquidity Risk, Haircut, and Correlation Governor as distinct examples; each has its own scope and assumptions.
